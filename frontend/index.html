What is Scrawdle?

Scrawdle is a drawing game. It hands you a random mix of animal parts, and your job is to draw the creature they make together.

How to play

  1. Pick Basic (head, arms, legs, tail, plus an optional extra) or Detailed (all of that, plus ears, eyes, mouth and an optional skin pattern).
  2. Watch the slot machine spin. Whatever it lands on is your set of parts.
  3. Your parts stay in the top-left corner while you draw. Use your mouse or finger on the canvas.
  4. Parts marked in green are optional: use them if you like, skip them if you don't.
  5. When you're done, judge your own drawing: Accurate if it matches your parts (you win!), Inaccurate if it doesn't.
  6. Want a different creature? Hit New set to go back and spin again.
