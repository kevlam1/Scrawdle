How to play

Scrawdle hands you a random mix of animal parts, and your job is to draw the creature they make together.

  1. Open Daily Scrawl. Everyone gets the same parts each day: a head, arms, legs and tail, plus ears, eyes and a mouth, with an optional extra, skin pattern and accessory.
  2. Watch the slot machine spin. Whatever it lands on is your set of parts.
  3. Your parts stay above the canvas while you draw. Parts marked optional are yours to use or skip.
  4. Draw with your mouse, finger or stylus. Your drawing saves in this browser as you go.
  5. Press Done drawing to publish it, download it, or watch it replay.
  6. In today's gallery, give other players' drawings a thumbs up or thumbs down. Once anyone gives your published drawing a thumbs up, you win!

Want a different creature? Free Play gives you random parts any time.

Daily Free Play has no parts: draw anything you like. It saves in your browser and clears each day, like the others.

Public drawings shows what everyone has posted today, and starts over each day. Daily Scrawl's drawings unlock once you post yours. Multiplayer Games makes a private lobby you can invite friends to.
