How to play

Scrawdle hands you a random mix of animal parts, and your job is to draw the creature they make together.

  1. Open Daily, then Daily Scrawl. Everyone gets the same parts each day: a head, arms, legs and tail, plus ears, eyes and a mouth, with an optional extra, skin pattern and accessory.
  2. Watch the slot machine spin. Whatever it lands on is your set of parts.
  3. Your parts sit on the Mission briefing note beside the easel. Tick each one off as you draw it; parts marked optional are yours to use or skip.
  4. Draw with your mouse, finger or stylus. Your drawing saves in this browser as you go.
  5. Press Done drawing to publish it, download it, or watch it replay.
  6. On Public drawings, tap the heart on the drawings you like. Once anyone likes your published drawing, you win!

Daily Doodle has no parts: draw anything you like today.

Free Play gives you random parts any time, as often as you like.

Time Challenge gives you four random parts and 30, 60 or 90 seconds. The canvas locks at 0:00.

Multiplayer makes a private lobby you can invite friends to, for Scrawdle Party.

Public drawings shows what everyone has posted today; drawings leave at midnight. Daily Scrawl's drawings unlock once you post yours.
