What is Scrawdle?

Scrawdle is a drawing game. It hands you a random mix of animal parts, and your job is to draw the creature they make together.

How to play

  1. Everyone gets the same parts each day. Pick Basic (head, arms, legs, tail, plus an optional extra) or Detailed (all of that, plus ears, eyes, mouth and an optional skin pattern).
  2. Watch the slot machine spin. Whatever it lands on is your set of parts.
  3. Your parts stay in the top-left corner while you draw. Use your mouse, finger or stylus on the canvas, and pick a tool, color and size above it.
  4. Your drawing saves in this browser as you go. Hit Done to see it finished and download it.
  5. Parts marked in green are optional: use them if you like, skip them if you don't.
  6. When you're done, see what other people drew for the same parts.
  7. Want a different creature? Free play gives you random parts any time.
