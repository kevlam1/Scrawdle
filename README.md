# Scrawdle

A daily drawing game. Every day everyone gets the same set of animal parts (Basic or Detailed) to combine into a creature, or can just draw anything in Daily Free Play. Players draw in the browser, can replay and download their drawing, publish it, and see what other players drew that day. Friends can also meet in private lobbies with a 5-letter code. Built for artists and for anyone who wants a quick daily creative pastime.

TCSS 490 Artifact 4, built by a team of four: [kevlam1](https://github.com/kevlam1), [arcangieo](https://github.com/arcangieo), [trentenmuw](https://github.com/trentenmuw), [zainasuw](https://github.com/zainasuw).

**Live at [scrawdle.vercel.app](https://scrawdle.vercel.app).** The build log is a page at `scrawdle.vercel.app/logs` (every entry of `log.md`, with who wrote it and a one-sentence summary), linked from the bottom of the game's home screen ("Read our build log"). It goes live with the first deploy that includes `frontend/logs/`; once it does, check that `/logs` loads without a trailing slash.

**Built**

- **Daily parts:** everyone playing on the same date gets the same ones, with no server needed. Free play gives random parts any time.
- **Drawing tools:** pen, pencil, line, eraser, paint bucket, and a brush with seven types (round, marker, calligraphy, crayon, watercolor, spray, dry bristle). 12 colors plus a custom color from Scrawdle's own color picker (a shade square, a hue bar, a hex box and the last six custom colors, the same in every browser, `frontend/color-picker.js`), a size for each tool, illustrated tool icons, a Brushes panel that previews each brush, and undo and redo.
- **The drawing screen** looks like an arcade cabinet (`frontend/arcade.css`): the parts on a pinned sticky note with a box to tick for each, the tools as chunky keys, the canvas in a wooden frame on an easel with the colors standing as markers on its tray, Small / Medium / Large as cards, and a control deck with a joystick, Undo / Redo / Clear and a big Done drawing. It has a light and a dark version, keeps its proportions at any window size or browser zoom, and on phones keeps the canvas on the first screen with Done drawing pinned to the bottom. Scrawdle Party's drawing screen keeps the earlier look.
- **Today's leaderboard** at `/leaderboard/` (linked from the home screen, the finish screen, and Public drawings): today's published drawings ranked by likes (the heart), one list for Daily Scrawl and one for Daily Free Play, with the top three as a podium, places 4 to 10 below (everyone tied for 10th shows), and "Yours is #4 of 23 today" for your own. Drawings with no likes aren't ranked; ties share a place, and older thumbs-down votes still in the database break a tie. Daily Scrawl stays locked until you've posted yours, like on Public drawings. It ranks drawings, not players, since a published drawing carries no name. It resets at midnight with everything else, and needs no database change (`frontend/leaderboard.js`).
- **Time Challenge** (a card on the home screen): four random parts (head, arms, legs, tail), a choice of 30, 60 or 90 seconds on the reveal screen (remembered), "3, 2, 1, Draw!", then a clock beside the parts that turns red for the last ten seconds. The canvas locks at 0:00 ("Time's up!") and the finish screen says "Drawn in 60 seconds!" or "Finished in 42 of 60 seconds!"; Done drawing finishes early. It has its own save slot, so it never replaces your Free Play drawing, and goes to My drawings when a new challenge replaces it. Leaving the drawing or reloading ends it, keeping what was drawn. It isn't published and doesn't count toward the streak. The clock is `frontend/timer.js`, shared with Scrawdle Party.
- **Yesterday's winner:** the drawing with the most likes yesterday (by your local date), for Daily Scrawl and for Daily Free Play, above today's lists on the leaderboard (it was moved off the home screen so it doesn't sit over the game's buttons); everyone tied for first shows (up to three on the leaderboard), and it says "🏆 Your drawing won yesterday!" when it's yours. It uses yesterday's drawings, which the daily wipe already keeps for that long, so nothing extra is stored. Needs database update `005-yesterdays-winner.sql`, which Kevin reported running on the live project on 2026-11-07; a winner (with 3 likes) showed on the leaderboard on 2026-11-08.
- **Streak:** how many days in a row you've finished a daily drawing (Daily Scrawl or Daily Free Play; not Free Play or a blank canvas), shown once it reaches 3 days in a row: under the date on the home screen ("5-day streak", with "Draw today to keep it going" until today's is done), on the finish screen ("That's 5 days in a row!", or a new best), and at the end of the share text. Days 1 and 2 count but don't show yet. Kept in this browser only (`frontend/streak.js`).
- **Auto-save in the browser:** Basic/Detailed (one drawing a day), Daily Free Play, and Free play each save separately and survive a refresh. When the day changes, all three move to My drawings, where they can be downloaded.
- **Finish screen** with a PNG download that includes the prompts and date, and **Replay**, which redraws the drawing stroke by stroke in about 2 to 4 seconds, ending on an exact match.
- **Likes as the win condition:** on Public drawings, players tap a small heart in the corner of other players' drawings (one like each, which they can take back); it fills red and shows how many players like the drawing. A published drawing that gets at least one like has won, and its player sees "You won!" and its heart count on their own card. Scrawdle Party uses the same heart. In the database a like is still a vote of 1 (older thumbs-down votes of -1 stay, but there's no button for them now). This replaced the self-judged Accurate/Inaccurate buttons. Needs database update `002-votes.sql` (see below); until it's run, the gallery shows no vote buttons. There's no login, so a voter is a random id kept in the browser.
- **Daily Free Play:** (internal mode id `scrawl`; the name "Daily Scrawl" is saved for the planned Basic + Detailed merge, see `CLAUDE.md`) a free draw with no parts and no slot machine. It has its own save slot and publishes to the gallery like the others.
- **Publishing and the gallery:** a finished drawing can be published to Supabase and shown to other players that day (`frontend/publish.js`). The sample drawings that used to open from the home and finish screens are gone.
- **Daily wipe:** old drawings' files and rows are deleted by a scheduled Supabase function at 10:00 UTC every day; lobby codes older than a day are deleted too (`backend/README.md`).
- **Lobbies:** Create Lobby, then Invite friends sends a link like `scrawdle.vercel.app/?BCDFG` (the share menu on phones; Text message, WhatsApp, Email, or Copy invite elsewhere). Players never see or type a code. A lobby room shows who's in it, with a host and up to 8 players, and has a small contract for games to plug into (`frontend/lobby.js`). The host can kick a player (from the lobby room, or from a Players button during a game), and with 3 or more players anyone can start a vote to kick someone, which passes only if everyone else agrees. A kicked player can't rejoin that lobby from the same browser for the rest of the day.
- **Scrawdle Party,** the first lobby game (`frontend/party.js`): the host picks Basic or Detailed parts, 1, 3 or 5 rounds, and 60, 90 or 120 seconds. Each round everyone draws the same random parts with the full drawing tools, then likes the other drawings with the heart, names hidden; results show who drew what, and each like is a point. Players who join mid-round sit it out, players who leave keep their points, and the game ends if the host leaves. Drawings and votes only travel as lobby messages and are never stored. Needs at least 2 players.

## Stack

- **Frontend:** plain HTML5 Canvas and vanilla JavaScript, no framework and no build step. The parts to draw are in `frontend/prompts.json`, a plain file anyone can edit, not in the database.
- **Backend:** Supabase on the free plan: Postgres and Storage for drawings and lobby codes, Realtime for lobbies, and an Edge Function with `pg_cron` for the daily wipe. The team chose it over Firebase because its free plan can run the scheduled wipe. There's no login: the public key is safe in the page because Row Level Security limits what it can do (see `backend/README.md`).
- **Hosting:** Vercel, serving the `frontend/` folder (`vercel.json`). Every push to `main` deploys.
- **Build log:** a small Node script generates `frontend/logs/index.html` from `log.md`.

## Running locally

There's no build step:

- Serve the frontend folder (opening `index.html` straight from disk won't work, because the page loads `prompts.json` with `fetch`): from the repo root, run `npx serve frontend` (or `python -m http.server 8000 --directory frontend`), then open the printed address.
- You should see the home screen with Daily (Daily Scrawl and Daily Free Play), Multiplayer, Free Play and Time Challenge. Pick Daily Scrawl to spin the slot machine, then draw on the canvas with a mouse, finger, or stylus.
- Tests: `node --test tests/*.test.js` runs every test file (`syntax` checks that every browser script parses; `color-picker` covers the custom color picker's color math; `purge` covers the daily-wipe logic with a fake Supabase client; `lobby` covers code parsing, host choice, and names; `votes` covers the win rule and changing a vote; `party-rules` covers Scrawdle Party's scoring and rounds; `publish-votes` runs the real `publish.js` against an in-memory stand-in for Supabase; `logs-page` fails if `frontend/logs/index.html` is out of date with `log.md`).
- The logs page is generated: after editing `log.md`, add a summary for any new entry to `scripts/log-summaries.mjs` and run `node scripts/make-logs-page.mjs`.
- To try a lobby, open the page in two windows (one normal, one private) and join the same code. It needs the lobby update in `backend/supabase/updates/` to have been run on the Supabase project (it was, on Kevin's).

There's nothing to install. `frontend/supabase-config.js` holds the real project's URL and publishable key (a plain committed JS file, not a `.env`, because there's no build step; see `backend/README.md` for why that's safe). **That means a local copy publishes to the same real Supabase project as the live site**, so test drawings and lobbies you create while developing show up there until the daily jobs remove them. To work without publishing, temporarily empty the two values in `supabase-config.js` (the Publish button, real gallery, and Lobby card then stay hidden), and don't commit that change.
